# Figure preview check

Open the preview (⇧⌘V) — the portrait should float right with an italic caption beneath it.

^^^
![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d3/Albert_Einstein_Head.jpg/220px-Albert_Einstein_Head.jpg){.figure-right width=220}
^^^ Albert Einstein, c. 1947

Body text wraps around the floated figure rather than starting below it. Without the extension
installed, the `^^^` lines show as literal text and the `{…}` attributes are ignored, which is the
before-and-after worth checking.
